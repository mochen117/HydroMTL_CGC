- batch size: 64
- joint/fine-tune epochs: 100
- Q-pretrain epochs: 200
- base loss: RMSE
- Q/SSM loss weights: 0.5 / 0.5
- validation: disabled
- early stopping: disabled

Important missing-data semantics:
